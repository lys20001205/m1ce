# V13 R13: engine service guidance after cargo collection

During an ordinary R12 touch playthrough, returning to the engine with two loaded crates displayed “上车顶接桥” on the interaction button, while tapping it opened SPEED. The cargo cue also persisted over the open console. The nearby ARMORY had the same mismatch. These were recorded during the second and third runs of an earned, persistent save, without test fixtures or simulation speed changes.

When empty-handed at an available engine service near a connected depot, the navigation now names SPEED or ARMORY and clears the competing ladder marker. An open console explains that CRUISE leaves the station. Cargo, rooftop, held-crate, damaged-equipment and floor-pickup behavior retains its existing priority. This changes guidance only; movement, interaction rules, cargo value, weapon prices, permanent progression and saves are unchanged.

The bounded review also checked visible rewards, weapon choice and progression. The first defensive run earned 2,400; the next run delivered two crates and earned 3,300. Normally purchased upgrades visibly supplied a handgun, 6 Scrap and 210 engine HP on the next start, then a selectable shotgun and 3.6 m/s carrying speed on the following start. Shop reading paused simulation. These are AI-operated browser observations, not a human enjoyment study or a physical-phone performance claim. No economy or new-content change was justified.

Validation includes executable service/exception unit cases, rendered touch-button and panel assertions, and independent normal-entry replay of the affected interaction. The release requires the unchanged complete dual-browser CI gates and public resource/startup/cache checks against the merged commit.
