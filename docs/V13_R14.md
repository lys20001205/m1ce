# V13 R14: comparable weapon choices

The R13 armory explained weapons in English, while departure choices showed only range and magazine size. In an ordinary earned-save inspection at 812×332, neither departure choice exposed the shotgun's damage falloff or reload time. The existing panels scrolled correctly; this was an information gap, not a clipping defect.

Both menus now use one Chinese summary with the existing weapon identifier, a brief role or limitation, and range plus reload/magazine or melee attack interval taken directly from the balance configuration. The shotgun identifies five pellets and falloff after 4m; the rifle identifies up to three pierced enemies with diminishing damage. Shop prices and owned/selected states remain in the same buttons. No simulation, economy, progression, save format, assets, or persistent HUD layout changes.
