# V11 QA follow-up C04 — close-contact ranged collision

## Reproduced defect

S2 BUG, observed in a recorded TEST-FIXTURE browser encounter; not a blind first-time playtest.
Baseline production commit: `c79176e90782ef4968301cad3b1923cd9e7e3e61`.
Exploration source: `bdd83d8546201d1aac898cdc126c5d761d286bfc` (only QA tooling differs).
Run: `35479869887`, WebKit artifact `10595681283`, SHA-256
`ab4271dd1a7287c6ae6c9f4d48da395e8960b8e585a057e0fe71476d827d8bf0`.

The comparison placed the player at x=1.7, facing right, with Rifle, and a Boarder at x=3.
After 2.1 seconds of real held K input, the Boarder still had 45 HP and the player had
91 HP. Recorded muzzle x=3.3585 was beyond the approaching enemy x=2.86875.
Screenshot: `probe-edges-webkit-33-weapon-rifle.png`, with continuous recording and state log.
The bullet previously checked collisions only forward from the muzzle, so the enemy
between the player and that muzzle was missed. Unit reproduction extends the same
root cause to both facings and shorter distances for all three ranged weapons.
The browser observation does not claim that Handgun/SMG failed at the Rifle's 1.3m distance.

## Minimal repair

On projectile creation, retain the player's firing x as a one-use launch coordinate.
The first collision sweep covers player-to-muzzle plus the first flight segment.
Subsequent sweeps use the previous projectile position as before. Visual muzzle,
range origin, velocity, damage, cooldown, weapons, currency, enemies and save schema
are unchanged. Targets behind the player, on another layer, boarding, or changing
layers remain excluded. The nearest eligible target is hit once; there is no piercing.
