# V13 R12: cargo guidance and scene readability

Phone screenshots showed three related presentation problems: cargo pickups repeated text over the carrier's head; saturated decorative containers competed with collectible crates; and the imported hostile characters retained nearly the same grey/pink body palette as the player.

R12 keeps cargo value and accounting in the existing HUD and receipt instead of repeating cargo text above the carrier. At depots, the contact ring and beacon identify the player, the immediate action moves right of the head, and distant cargo navigation yields to the HUD and carrier silhouette. No input target or collision changes.

Imported background scenery receives a subdued cool material treatment; hostile character bodies receive a warm rust tint. Derived materials are cached by source and visual role. Original GLB materials, player, collectible crates, train, textures, geometry, enemy role equipment and licensed source files remain unchanged. There are no new external assets.

Independent art review compared eight fixed scenes at 844×390 and 812×332: carrying and loading cargo, industrial enemies and tunnel enemies. The first candidate still covered the short-screen carrier's head; the second candidate moved the depot action aside and passed the eight-image review. Screenshot fixtures are isolated test states, not ordinary play or physical-phone evidence. Runtime touch and complete release validation are separate gates.

The economy, save schema, earned weapons, permanent growth and simulation rules are unchanged. Original public URL and cache recovery verification remain in place. Browser-based touch and screenshots cannot establish physical-phone brightness, touch latency, colour accessibility or human enjoyment.
