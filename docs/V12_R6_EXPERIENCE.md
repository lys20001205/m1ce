# V12 R6c: departure choices and cargo visibility

Normal play on R4d and R5b exposed a career that could reach its seven-level cap after a few payouts, foreground posts obscuring characters/cargo, and an off-center station bridge with unclear instructions after returning to the engine roof. R5b's shop pause, shipment accounting and individual firearm magazines remain in place.

The permanent departure license now has three levels: 900 Bank unlocks the handgun, 1400 adds the shotgun choice, and 2400 adds SMG and rifle choices. Each new run starts with **one selected gun**, the wrench and six Scrap. Other guns remain normal run purchases; choosing rifle does not grant intermediate-tier guns. Selecting a departure weapon is free, restricted to preparation/results, and saved under the existing release save namespace. Old Bank, preparation items and seven-level careers are preserved. Boots and hull prices are unchanged; this adds different starting play styles rather than a higher cost for the same numerical upgrade. The career totals nine levels and remains a small prototype progression, not a large endgame system.

The renderer derives a cutaway of camera-facing flatbed posts and elevated depot rails from the existing official Kenney CC0 geometry. The source GLBs, textures, wheels, rear rails, floor and simulation collisions are retained. The depot's authored walking deck is aligned with its gameplay floor. Derived geometry is shared across rebuilds to keep the GPU geometry count bounded. No new asset pack, dependency, physics or reward rule is introduced.

Cargo navigation uses the actual central bridge and nearest usable cargo car. Off-center bridges identify the yellow ladder/roof path, then the bridge; returning onto the engine roof directs the carrier toward the cargo hatch and LOAD. The HUD keeps stock, carried value and the fact that an unladen crate is not credited. The marker is omitted when already at the usable action, and is clipped inside the viewport. New labels are synchronized with V12 R6c, BUILD, manifest start URL and authored release cache identity.

Validation: 397 unit tests passed, including old-save migration, paid license selection/reload, individual attack behavior, actual GLB cutaway/floor/shared geometry and an off-center enter/pickup/return/move/LOAD transition. A separate fresh release entry used only ordinary keys and DOM controls for the latter path: 53.8 seconds, nine screenshots, HP100, carried450/loaded0/unsettled1000 before LOAD and loaded450/unsettled1450 afterward. Browser fixtures remain separate from ordinary play. Mobile-art Chromium63/WebKit58, packaging11/browser and cache11/browser passed; the design suite's new stock-and-action caption assertion preserves the stock value and adds the required bridge action. The corrected design suite passed67/browser. Local full release-matrix environment limitations from R5b remain disclosed until the exact R6c CI result is known. No physical-phone heat/gesture or subjective sound validation is claimed.

The informed AI continuous play reports and screenshots are kept locally under `artifacts/overall-r6c` and `artifacts/offcenter-r6c`; these are not human blind play. Independent candidate QA and exact-commit CI remain required before publishing. A good test result does not establish that the whole game is fun: stationary engine defense and long-term replay variety still require broader human feedback.
