# Assets actually used in ROUNDHOUSE V11

- `train-cutaway.json`, `crew-robot.json`, `cargo-crate.json`: original authored geometry generated by `tools/build.mjs`; no third-party art inputs.
- Route rings, Roundhouse gates, Depots, Industrial/Freight/Tunnel landmarks, weapon/enemy dressing, signs and effects: original procedural primitives in `src/routeworld.js`, `src/actors3d.js`, `src/stations3d.js`, `src/feedback3d.js` and `src/view.js`. Shared geometry/materials and enemy pooling are used at runtime.
- Audio: original oscillator envelopes and procedural noise in `src/audio.js`. No external audio samples, streams, microphone recording or third-party sound assets.
- Three.js 0.180.0: MIT. The exact version and SHA-512 integrity are pinned in `package-lock.json`; its full license ships at `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`. Upstream: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180
- System UI fonts only. No font files redistributed.

Kenney Factory Kit and Quaternius were considered but **not imported**. There are no runtime CDN art dependencies. This inventory describes used assets rather than granting licenses to unimported candidates.
