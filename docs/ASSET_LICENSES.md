# Assets used in ROUNDHOUSE V12

21 selected official Kenney GLB models ship locally under assets/kenney, together with each pack's original License.txt and its independent textures. Total selected assets/manifest/licenses: 1,240,516 bytes. Full download ZIPs do not ship; no runtime CDN.

| Official pack | Main replacements | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Train Kit 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/train-kit) | diesel locomotive, flatbed/cargo, connectors, near rails | CC0 |
| [Factory Kit 3.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/factory-kit) | roof/depot platforms, rails, supports, stairs, boxes, crane | CC0 |
| [City Kit Industrial 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial) | factories, shipping containers, water towers | CC0 |
| [Blocky Characters 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters) | animated red crew and purple enemy robot bodies | CC0 |

assets/kenney/manifest.json records official pages/download URLs, selected filenames, byte sizes and model SHA-256. Four original License.txt files explicitly identify Creative Commons Zero. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying, modification and commercial redistribution. Credit to Kenney is included voluntarily. Pack-specific Textures directories and character texture-g/h are preserved.

Original authored geometry remains for weapon/muzzle sockets, ladders, role equipment, signs, combat effects, distant silhouettes and explicitly warned missing-model fallback. Primary normal-play hulls/roofs/character bodies use GLB replacements. Robots remain deliberately blocky; this is not a realistic human makeover. Procedural audio and system fonts remain original/local.

Three.js 0.180.0, GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils are MIT; the bundled full license is vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Package-lock pins version/integrity. No Quaternius content, paid assets or account-gated downloads are included.
